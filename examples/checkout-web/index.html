TypeScript SDK · v0.3.0

Verify Ethiopian payment receipts.

Customers pay your bank account or wallet directly. Your Node.js backend accepts a Telebirr or CBE identifier, receipt link, or SMS. The SDK checks the official receipt against your order and records a claim in your shared store when it verifies.
