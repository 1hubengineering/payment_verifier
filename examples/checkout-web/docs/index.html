Payment Verifier developer guide

Customers pay your designated bank account or wallet directly. Your Node.js backend accepts a Telebirr or CBE identifier, official receipt link, or SMS, then checks the official receipt against your authorized order. A VERIFIED result records a receipt claim in your shared store at the applicable transaction commit.

Your application owns the database, orders, ledger and fulfillment. The SDK writes verification records through your configured adapter. See who owns each responsibility.

For the published 0.3.0 SDK, start with the offline example or build a persistent checkout. Common questions and the API reference cover individual tasks.

An inconclusive result does not show that the customer failed to pay. Recover without asking for another payment.
